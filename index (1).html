LinkDeck

Control your PC from your phone. Same Wi-Fi, no internet needed.

Version1.0.1
Phone appAndroid
PC agentWindows 10 / 11
NetworkHome Wi-Fi or PC hotspot

Download

1. On your PC

Installs the LinkDeck agent that your phone talks to.

Download for Windows
  1. Run LinkDeck-Setup.exe and approve the admin prompt.
  2. If Windows SmartScreen warns you, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open LinkDeck. It shows your PC address and a 6-digit pair code.

2. On your phone

The remote: touchpad, voice, screen, files and chat.

Download for Android
  1. Open LinkDeck.apk from your downloads.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
  3. Open LinkDeck from your apps.

Connect in 4 steps

  1. Put your phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi, or turn on the PC mobile hotspot and join it from your phone.
  2. Start LinkDeck on the PC and note the 6-digit code.
  3. In the app, pick Same Wi-Fi or PC hotspot, then tap Find my PC.
  4. Enter the code and tap Pair and connect. Your phone stays paired after that.

What you can do

PadMouse, keyboard, media keys, shortcuts
VoiceSay "Jarvis, open Chrome"
ScreenSee your PC and tap to click
FilesSend and fetch files both ways
ChatGroup chat for everyone connected
PowerLock, sleep, restart, shut down

Help

"Find my PC" does not find my PC

Check LinkDeck is running on the PC and that both devices are on the same network. Some public or campus Wi-Fi blocks devices from seeing each other. In that case, type the address shown on the PC (for example 192.168.137.1, without the port) into the app, or use the PC hotspot.

The PC hotspot will not start

Windows needs an active connection to share. Connect the PC to Wi-Fi or Ethernet first, or use a phone hotspot and join the PC to it.

Windows shows a warning about the installer

The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows a caution screen. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Does voice need internet?

Only once. The first time voice starts, the PC downloads a small speech model. After that, speech is recognised on your PC and works offline.

Wrong code or "too many tries"

Each pair code works once, and the PC shows a fresh one after every pairing. After 5 wrong tries a device is blocked for a minute.

Use it on networks you trust. LinkDeck can control your whole PC, and traffic on your local network is not encrypted. Only pair phones you own.
